Apple Crumble

by Asher Sterling

    Filling Ingredients:
  1. 8 apples, peeled, cored, and diced
  2. 1/2 cup brown sugar
  3. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  4. 2 tsp cinnamon powder
  5. A pinch of salt

    6. Topping Ingredients:
  6. 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  7. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  8. 1/2 cup brown sugar
  9. 1/2 cup unsalted butter, chilled and cut into small pieces
  10. 1 tsp cinnamon powder

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (180°C).
2. Lightly grease a 9x9-inch baking dish with either non-stick spray or softened butter and keep it ready.
3. Wash, peel, core and dice the apples and place them into a large bowl.
4. In the large bowl, mix together the diced apples, brown sugar, flour, cinnamon, and nutmeg until the apples are uniformly coated. Then, pour this apple blend into your prepared baking dish, spreading it out evenly.
5. In another bowl, add the oats, flour, brown sugar, butter chunks, cinnamon, nutmeg, and a dash of salt. Blend using a fork or pastry cutter until you get a crumbly texture with butter evenly incorporated.
6. Evenly spread the crumbly topping over the apple layer in the baking dish. To smooth it out, gently pat it down with a spatula or the rear side of a spoon.
7. Transfer the dish into the oven. Bake for around 40-45 minutes, or until you notice the topping turning a golden shade and the apple layer bubbling at the sides.
8. After baking, set the apple crisp aside to cool for a bit. It's best served warm, either solo or with a dollop of vanilla bean ice-cream.
