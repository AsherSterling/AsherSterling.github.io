Apple Crumble

by Asher Sterling

Filling Ingredients:
1. 8 apples, peeled, cored, and diced
2. 1/2 cup brown sugar
3. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
4. 2 tsp cinnamon powder
Topping Ingredients:
1. 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
2. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
3. 1/2 cup brown sugar
4. 1/2 cup unsalted butter, chilled and cut into small pieces
5. 1 tsp cinnamon powder
