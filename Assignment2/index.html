Apple Crumble

A baking tutorial
by Asher Sterling

Filling Ingredients:

  1. 6 apples, peeled, cored, and diced
  2. 1/2 cup brown sugar
  3. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  4. 2 tsp cinnamon powder

Topping Ingredients:

  1. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  2. 1/2 cup brown sugar
  3. 120g unsalted butter, melted but chilled
  4. 1 tsp cinnamon powder
  5. a pinch of salt

Recipe:

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Lightly grease a 9x9-inch baking dish with either non-stick spray or softened butter and keep it ready.
  3. Melt the prepared butter on medium heat before setting it aside to chill.
  4. Wash, peel, core and dice the apples and place them into a large bowl.
  5. In the large bowl, mix together the diced apples, brown sugar, flour, and cinnamon until the apples are uniformly coated. Then, pour this apple mix into the 9 by 9 inch pan, spreading it out evenly.
  6. In a seperate bowl add flour, brown sugar, butter chunks, cinnamon, and a dash of salt. Blend using a fork or pastry cutter until you get a crumbly texture with butter evenly incorporated.
  7. Evenly spread the crumbly topping over the apple layer in the baking dish. To smooth it out, gently pat it down with a spatula or the rear side of a spoon.
  8. Transfer the baking pan into the oven. Bake for around 40-45 minutes, or until you notice the topping turning a golden shade and the apple layer bubbling at the sides.
  9. After baking, set the apple crumble aside to cool for a bit. It's best served warm, either solo or with a dollop of vanilla bean ice-cream.
