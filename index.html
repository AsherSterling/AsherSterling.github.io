Interactive Experience Review of Endless Summer by Richard Mille

1. What was the first thing you paid attention to when interacting with the experience?

What grabbed my attention at first was the stylized 3D animation of the experience. It showcased a consistent beautiful view of what seems to be summer.

2. Spend two minutes with the experience and create a list of each of your discrete actions.

Clicking across the screen to activate and start the user journey Resizing the screen to that of a mobile shaped, which finally started the user journey

3. What was the most common action in your two minute interaction with the experience?

I was constantly moving around the minimized screen to look at the full view of the looped animation.

4. What part of the experience did you spend the most time engaging with?

I spent most of my time engaging with the scroll wheel at the bottom of the screen, moving it to change the scenery and time shown on the screen.

5. What is your impression of the intended primary goal of the interactive experience?

It was not clear what the intended primary goal was throughout my experience interacting with the website.

6. How does the interactive experience communicate this primary goal?

While on my run with the interactive experience I had no idea that the primary goal was to promote a watch of a new series by Richard Mille.

7. What is your impression of how the experience should be interacted with over time? (For how long and how many different times)

I feel as though it should be interacted with once and slowly. Looking through each section with intent and curiosity.

8. How does the interactive experience communicate how it should be interacted with over time?

The interactive experience communicated well how it should be interacted with by displaying simple instructions and highlighting important buttons and interactive media.

9. What other media forms (digital or otherwise) does the experience reference?

Stylized 3D animated loops, images, possibly Virtual Reality, Blender, City Pop Artstyle,

10. What does this reference/s communicate to you about how you should act when engaging with your research experience?

11. What does this reference/s communicate to you about how you should feel when engaging with your research experience?

12. What is the most frustrating part of the interaction to you and what makes that part frustrating?

The part that I found most frustrating during my interaction was that the only to move forward in the story was through click-dragging the scroll wheel. I wanted to skip a few sections and there was no easier way to do so.

13. What is the most satisfying part of the interaction to you and what makes that part satisfying?

I think for me, the most satisfying part of the whole interactive experience was the poetic interlude added to enhance the immersion of the user. It makes it more enjoyable and leaves a more impression.
